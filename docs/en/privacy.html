Website visits and technical log data

The static pages are provided through GitHub Pages. When a page is accessed, GitHub and the hosting infrastructure may process technical connection data, in particular IP address, time, requested URL, referrer, browser and device information, and status and security data. This processing is used to deliver the website, maintain stability, troubleshoot errors and prevent abusive access.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is operating a secure, stable and functional website. We do not set a separate retention period for hosting-provider logs; retention follows the providers’ operational, security and retention rules. Further information is available in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Enquiry form

When you submit an enquiry, we process your name, email address, selected topic, message and the time of submission. Fields marked as required are needed to identify and respond to your enquiry. Without them, the form cannot be submitted and we cannot handle the enquiry. Using the form is voluntary. Please do not include health data or other particularly sensitive information.

The required checkbox confirms that you have read this notice. It is not a separate consent and is not stored separately in the CMS. Your enquiry is sent to Hawo Abdulle and Furat Abdulle for handling. Your email address is used as the reply-to address; no automatic confirmation email is sent.

For steps taken at your request, including preparation for possible collaboration, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Other enquiries are handled on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is responding to enquiries, organising communication and tracking their status.

Cloudflare, the CMS and email notifications

The enquiry is processed by a Cloudflare Worker, stored in a Cloudflare D1 database and sent as a notification to the people handling it through Cloudflare Email Service. The form data are not sent to GitHub Pages. Cloudflare processes the data as a technical service provider under the applicable service and data protection terms. The internal CMS is protected by Cloudflare Access, and access to stored enquiries is limited to authorised people.

Cloudflare and GitHub may process data in the United States or other countries outside the European Economic Area. Where a transfer to a third country takes place, it is based, under the provider’s applicable terms, on an adequacy decision (including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified and covered) or appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Cloudflare describes its transfer mechanisms in its Data Processing Addendum. GitHub’s information about international transfers is available in its privacy statement. The specific terms depend on the service and account in use.

Cloudflare Turnstile security check

Cloudflare Turnstile starts only when you submit the enquiry form. To prevent automated and abusive submissions, Turnstile processes technical signals, including IP address, TLS fingerprint, browser identifier (User-Agent), site key and origin. The legal basis for our processing is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is protecting the form against automated and abusive submissions. Cloudflare processes these signals on our behalf to provide the security check. Cloudflare may also use signals under its own responsibility to improve bot detection.

We do not use our own analytics or advertising cookies. Turnstile loads only to protect the form you requested; Cloudflare describes the signals it processes for this purpose as security data necessary for bot detection. Information about possible cookies and technical access to your device under Section 25 TDDDG is available in the Turnstile Privacy Addendum, Cloudflare’s Cookie Policy and the text of Section 25 TDDDG. Further information about Cloudflare’s general privacy practices is available in Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Retention

Enquiries are deleted from the CMS when they are no longer needed for handling. While an enquiry is being handled, it remains in the CMS. Once it is marked as closed, it is automatically deleted from the database no later than 180 days after that date; it may be deleted earlier. Email notifications are not automatically deleted after the same period. The people handling enquiries delete them once they are no longer needed, unless legal retention obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims require further storage. Service-provider logs are retained under the providers’ respective retention and security rules.

Your rights

Subject to the legal requirements, you have the right to access, correct and erase your data, restrict processing and receive your data in a portable format. Where processing is based on legitimate interests, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation. If processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent for the future. The processing of enquiries is not based on consent.

To exercise your rights, contact Hawo Abdulle at the postal or email address above. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. For Hesse, this includes the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (HBDI), Postfach 3163, 65021 Wiesbaden, Germany.

Automated decisions

No decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR, is made in connection with the enquiry form.
