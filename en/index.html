Her work

What makes
access possible

Hawo Abdulle led the International Women’s Café in Groß-Gerau for many years. Today she provides counselling on female genital mutilation/cutting (FGM/C) at FIM in Frankfurt and is a member of the Halima film collective.

Groß-Gerau

Community

Over breakfast at the Women’s Café, women talked about school, health and dealing with public authorities. When the group could no longer meet for breakfast during the pandemic, Abdulle organised and led an exercise session at the Anne Frank Youth Centre.

In 2016, her community work was recognised at the regional German Citizens’ Award ceremony.

Frankfurt am Main

Counselling

At FIM – Frauenrecht ist Menschenrecht e. V. (Women’s Rights Are Human Rights), Abdulle is a point of contact for FGM/C with a focus on Somalia. She advocates for women to be able to discuss intimate health concerns in a language they feel comfortable speaking.

Halima Collective

Film

With the Halima Collective, Abdulle helped make a film in which women share their experiences of FGM/C, forced displacement and life in Germany. She co-wrote the script and interpreted on set.

About the film ↓

The film project

HALIMA

How to Stay Strong

After surgery, Halima takes a regional train home. She talks about living with the effects of FGM/C, fleeing her home country and going about her daily life in Germany. Her character brings together the experiences of several women.
