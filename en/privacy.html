Privacy

Privacy notice.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Controller

Hawo Abdulle is responsible for processing the data submitted through the enquiry form. Contact: hawoo.abdulle@gmail.com.

Data we process

When you submit the form, we process your name, email address, selected topic, message and the time of submission. Please do not include health data or other particularly sensitive information.

Your enquiry is sent to Hawo Abdulle and Furat Abdulle for handling and stored in the protected CMS database. Your email address is used as the reply-to address; no automatic confirmation email is sent.

Purpose and legal basis

We use the data only to read and respond to your enquiry and manage its status. Depending on the enquiry, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual steps or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for handling other enquiries.

Service providers and transfers

A Cloudflare Worker processes the enquiry and stores it in Cloudflare D1. Cloudflare Email Service sends the notification to the people handling the enquiry and processes the email address and message content as a technical service provider.

The remaining static pages are delivered through GitHub Pages. Form data are sent directly to the Cloudflare API and are not submitted to GitHub Pages. When a page is accessed, the respective providers may process technical connection data such as IP address, time, requested URL and browser information to deliver and secure the service.

Cloudflare Turnstile helps prevent automated submissions. It may process technical signals such as IP address, browser identifiers, TLS fingerprint, site key and origin. For more information, see Cloudflare Turnstile's privacy notice and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

For more information about data processing by Cloudflare, see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Retention

Enquiries remain in the CMS while they are being handled. Once an enquiry is marked as closed, it is automatically deleted from the database after 180 days; it can be deleted earlier in the CMS. Notification copies in the handlers' email inboxes are subject to their own retention and deletion practices.

Your rights

Where provided by law, you may request access, correction, erasure or restriction of processing, and object to processing. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. For privacy requests, use the enquiry form and select “Data protection” as the topic.

Voluntary submission and security

Using the form is voluntary. Without the required information, we cannot handle the enquiry. Data are transmitted over HTTPS, and access to stored enquiries is limited to authorised people.
